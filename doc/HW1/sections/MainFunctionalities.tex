\section{Main Functionalities}
%What are the main functionalities of the web app? what services does it offer and how it is organized?


This web application will primarily allow registered customers-users to browse the bar's available products, including drinks and food and place orders online with ease.\\
Different features and actions may be available depending on the type of user account (e.g. regular customers or admin/staff users).\\
Some examples of this features are the possibility to buy dishes from the dashboard, to update the product catalogue, to manage incoming, completed orders and products availability, all with the goal of improving service efficiency. Every feature will be described better in the following chapters with the use of mock-up images, diagrams and REST-API calls examples.
